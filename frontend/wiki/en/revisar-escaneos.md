# Reviewing uncertain scans

The scanner isn't always sure. There are three cases where it asks for your help:

- **Low confidence**: less than 45% similarity with the best candidate.
- **Near-identical editions**: several printings of the same number (regular, alt-art, promos…) that look so alike it can't tell which one is yours.
- **In burst mode, between 45% and 65%**: it recognizes the card, but not well enough to add it on its own.

Those captures are left as **To review**.

## Picking the right card

Press **Review** on the session row, or **Fix** on a result. The picker opens:

- **Candidates:** up to 5 cards with their similarity percentage. Tap the right one.
- **Search:** “Search by name or number (e.g. BT25-120)…”, from 2 letters and up to 30 results, in case the right card isn't among the candidates.
- **If the top two share the same number**, all its printings are shown together: “It's **BT18-005 · Rush Attack SSB Vegeta**, but there are several printings: pick the edition of your card.” Below are the **Other candidates**, just in case.

> [!TIP]
> If your printing **doesn't exist** in the catalogue (rare promos, tournament prizes…), pick the same card in another printing. Your photo is saved and next time it'll recognize it.

## What happens when you confirm

1. **The scanner learns.** Your cropped photo is saved as **one more fingerprint** of that card (“Confirmed photo”), so it'll recognize it better next time. This is what improves accuracy the most over time, especially with alt-arts and promos.
2. **Inside a session** (burst or binder), the card **is added** to the session's location. If it had already been added as another card, the wrong one is removed and the right one is added.
3. **Outside a session** (card by card), confirming **only teaches** the scanner. To add the card, use **Add to collection**.

> [!WARNING]
> You can only confirm scans that **still have their photo**. Photos are deleted after 24 hours, so review uncertain ones the same day. After that, add or fix the card from [My collection](mi-coleccion.md).

## Fixing something already added

In the session panel, each added card has:
- **Fix**: pick the right card; the wrong one is removed and the right one is added;
- **Undo**: removes the added copy.

If you've already closed the session, find the card in [My collection](mi-coleccion.md) and remove the copy with **−**, or with **Move…** in its details.
