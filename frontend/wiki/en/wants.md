# Wants

**Wants** are the cards you **don't have and want to buy** (or want more copies of). Each want is for **one specific printing** (the version you mark, for example the SPR) and has **how many copies** you want.

## Adding a card to your wants

- **In Search:** every card has a **♡** in its top right corner. Tap it and the card goes to your wants with 1 copy (the heart turns into **♥**, with the number of copies if you want more than one). Tapping **♥** removes it.
- **In the card details:** under **Add to My collection** there's **♡ Add to my wants**. If it's already in your wants, it shows **♥ In my wants** with **− ×N +** to change how many copies you want and **Remove** to take it out.

> [!NOTE]
> Wants **don't change by themselves**: if you buy the card and add it to your collection, the want stays until you remove it.

## The Wants page

In the menu, **Wants** (on a phone, inside **More**), address `/wants`. It shows:
- the total: how many cards and copies you want and **roughly what they'd cost**, using each card's Cardmarket price (and how many have no price);
- each card with its image, number, rarity and price. If you already have copies, it says so: **You have 1 of this print** or **… of another print** (another printing of the same card);
- **− ×N +** and **Remove** on each card;
- the order: **Recently added** (newest first), **Card number** or **Price** (most expensive first).

Tapping the image opens the card details.

**Search:** the **Search your wants by name or number…** box filters your wants by name or number (the total at the top counts only the ones shown).

## Sharing them as a PDF (WhatsApp)

- **↗ Share PDF** (on a phone) makes the PDF and opens the phone's share menu: pick WhatsApp and the group.
- **⇩ Download PDF** downloads it (`my-wants-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`) to send it however you like.

The PDF has **what you see on the page**: with the search and order you chose. On A4 sheets of 12 cards, each with its image, how many copies you want (the orange pill), its name, number and rarity; at the top, the total cards and copies. **It has no prices.**

## Backup

Wants are part of the [collection backup](importar-exportar.md) (JSON). Restoring a backup replaces them with the ones in the backup; a backup from before wants existed leaves them alone.
