# The card page

When you click a card (in Search, My collection, News or Prices) its **card page** opens. It's laid out the way a player reads a card: **on the left, the physical card** (image, printings, your copies and its price) and **on the right, everything you need to play it**, in order of importance.

![BT9-101's page: the card and its printings, your copies and price on the left; legality, numbers, details, text with the erratum applied and Q&A on the right](../img/ficha-carta.jpg)

**To close it:** press **✕**, click outside the card page or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

**To go to the previous or next card** of your results without closing: the **‹ ›** arrows at the sides, or the <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> keys (they don't trigger while you're typing a note).

On a phone the card page fills the screen, with a bar at the top holding the card's name, **‹ ›** and **✕**; the card image is smaller, with its printings next to it, and your copies and the price come after the Q&A.

## Left column: the physical card

### The image and its sides

**Double-sided** cards have two buttons above the image: **Front** and **Awaken** for Leaders (**Back** for other double-sided cards). Switching side changes the image, the name, the power and the text.

![A Leader on its Awaken side](../img/ficha-leader.jpg)

### Printings with the same number

If a number has several printings (normal, alt-art, SPR, tournament promos…), **Printings of BT20-149** appears under the image, with a thumbnail of each one and its rarity. The one you're viewing is highlighted; tap another to open it. Hovering shows its link, rarity and set.

### Your copies

Under the image is the panel with **your copies** of that card. It says **Not in your collection** or **In your collection: ×3**, plus “1 fake” if you have any fakes.

![Copies panel on the card page, inside My collection](../img/coleccion-ficha.jpg)

**One row per location**: copies in a deck are tagged **In deck**; the rest show their type (Binder, Storage). Fake copies go in their own row, marked **Fake**. In each row:
- **− / +**: removes or adds a copy in that location. Fake rows don't have **+**.
- **Mark as fake** / **It's genuine**: switches **one** copy from genuine to fake or the other way round. If the row has several copies and the note has positions (for example, from the binder), it asks **Which one is the fake?** and offers each position.
- **Move…**: moves copies to another location: “Move [n] to [location] → **Move**”.
- **Note**: free text of up to 500 characters. It's saved when you leave the field. Examples: “page 12” or, for a fake, “AliExpress, June”.

**Add to My collection in [location] → Add** adds a genuine copy in a location where you don't have that card yet. It suggests the last location you used in this browser, or **Unsorted**.

> [!NOTE]
> This panel only appears when the card is on the results page you have open. If you open it from a link or from the printings row and it isn't on that page, it doesn't show. Search for it and open it from the results.

Everything about copies, locations and fakes is in [My collection](mi-coleccion.md).

### The price

![Price box with its sources and links](../img/precio-ficha.jpg)

**The big figure:**
- **“€51.74”**: market price.
- **“from €11.60”**: there's only one seller's listing, with no real sales.

**Underneath, where it comes from:**
- **“market price · Cardmarket”** or **“· TCGplayer”**: based on real sales.
- **“price set by hand”**: you set it yourself in the [price review](revisar-precios.md).
- **“only a seller's asking price: no sales (not counted in the value)”**.
- **“doubtful price: a single listing, no sales (not counted in the value)”**: a listing out of proportion for its rarity, pending review.

**Sources line:** “Cardmarket €51.74 · TCGplayer €46.56 (US) · from €50.00 in Europe”. That way you can see at a glance whether the sources agree. **Change:** “7 days: ▲ 3%”, “30 days: ▼ 8%”.

**Links:** **Offers in Spain ↗** (Cardmarket, sellers from Spain, English cards), **Offers in Europe ↗** (all of Europe) and **TCGplayer ↗** (if the card is matched). If the card has no product on Cardmarket, the links go to a search by its name.

**Price history:** if the price counts towards the value, the chart comes underneath, with **30 days**, **90 days** (default) and **All**, how much it changed in that period and each day's price on hover. The history started on **28 October 2026**; until there's a second data point it says “History started on 28 Oct: the chart will fill in over the coming days.”

How each price is chosen, with all its rules, is in [Where each price comes from](precios.md).

## Right column: playing the card

### Name, number and link

At the top: the **name**, **number · set name** and some badges: **rarity**, **✎ Errata** (or “Errata: none”), **Q&A: N** and **🔗 Copy link**. The button copies this card's address; pasting it somewhere else opens this card page directly. Once copied it says **Link copied ✓**. If the browser won't let it copy, the link is shown selected so you can copy it yourself. See [Links, addresses and shortcuts](enlaces-y-navegacion.md#link-to-a-card).

### Can I play it?

Right under the name, a strip with an icon says whether the card can go in a deck:
- **✓ Legal · up to 4 copies** (green), with the formats it's legal in (Constructed · Deck Limited · Draft).
- **⚠** (amber) for a limit: **Limited · 1 copy max (deck + side deck)**, or a deck-building rule from its skills, for example **Max. 1 copy in the deck** for [Ultimate], [Super Combo] (4 max in the deck) or [Dragon Ball] (7 max in total).
- **⛔ Banned in official tournaments** (red): it can't go in the deck or the side deck.

Banned and Limited show the date from which they apply.

### Cost, Power and Combo

The numbers you build with, large and side by side:
- **Cost**: the energy cost with its **colour-cost dots** and, underneath, “2 red + 1 blue”. Z cards show their **Z-Energy** cost.
- **Power**: on Leaders it changes with the side (“front side” / “awakened”).
- **Combo**: the power it adds (“+10,000”) and what it costs (“costs 1 energy”), or “can't combo”.

### Details

Six tiles: **Type**, **Color**, **Rarity**, **Character**, **Special trait** and **Era**. **Type, Character, Special trait and Era are buttons**: tapping one filters Search by it.

### Card text

The card's current text, large and with each skill in its colour, as on the printed card: **Auto** blue, **Activate** orange, **Permanent** pink, keyword skills ([Barrier], [Blocker]…) red. Colour costs inside the text show as coloured dots.

- **Tap a skill** to see its official explanation in a small box; from there, **Filter cards with [Barrier] →** searches for cards with that skill. <kbd>Esc</kbd> or tapping elsewhere closes it.
- **⧉ Copy text** copies the text (with the erratum applied).

**Erratum:** if Bandai has published one, the text shown is already **the current wording**, with what changed **highlighted**, and a note above with its date and Bandai's explanation. **Show printed text** switches to the text as printed, with what was removed crossed out.

### Official Q&A

The official questions about the card, as **Q** (question) and **A** (answer), with the set they come from. The first two come open; tap a question to open or close it. The ones Bandai marks as recent carry the **new** tag. If there are none: “No official questions for this card yet.”

### Notes

At the end, Bandai's notes on the card, if there are any (for example, which products include it).
