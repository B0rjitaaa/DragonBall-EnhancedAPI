# The card page

When you click a card (in Search, My collection, News or Prices) its **card page** opens: the large image and everything known about it.

![BT20-149's page: image, printings with the same number, price, deck-building rules, text and attributes](../img/ficha-carta.jpg)

**To close it:** press **✕**, click outside the card page or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

## Left column

### The image and its sides

**Double-sided** cards (Leaders and a few others) have two buttons: **Front** and **Back**. Switching side changes the image, the name, the text and the attributes table.

![A Leader: Front / Back buttons](../img/ficha-leader.jpg)

### Printings with the same number

If a number has several printings (normal, alt-art, SPR, tournament promos…), **Printings of BT20-149** appears under the image, with a thumbnail of each one and its rarity. The one you're viewing is highlighted; tap another to open it. Hovering shows its link, rarity and set.

### Price history

If the card has a price that counts towards the value (with sales), the **Price history** chart appears below:

- **Periods:** **30 days**, **90 days** (default) and **All**.
- **Top right:** how much it has changed over that period (**▲ 12%**, **▼ 5%**, or **=** if it has moved less than 0.5%).
- **Tooltip:** hovering over the chart shows each day's price.

The history started on **28 October 2026**. While there's only one data point, instead of the chart it says “History started on 28 Oct: the chart will fill in over the coming days.”

## Right column

### Number, set and link

At the top: **number · set name** and the **🔗 Copy link** button. The button copies this card's address; pasting it somewhere else opens this card page directly. Once copied it says **Link copied ✓**. If the browser won't let it copy, the link is shown selected so you can copy it yourself. See [Links, addresses and shortcuts](enlaces-y-navegacion.md#link-to-a-card).

### The price

![Price box with its sources and links](../img/precio-ficha.jpg)

**The big figure:**
- **“€51.74”**: market price.
- **“from €11.60”**: there's only one seller's listing, with no real sales.

**Underneath, where it comes from:**
- **“market price · Cardmarket”** or **“· TCGplayer”**: based on real sales.
- **“price set by hand”**: you set it yourself in the [price review](revisar-precios.md).
- **“only a seller's asking price: no sales (not counted in the value)”**.
- **“doubtful price: a single listing, no sales (not counted in the value)”**: a listing out of proportion for its rarity, pending review.

**Sources line:** “Cardmarket €51.74 · TCGplayer €46.56 (US) · from €50.00 in Europe”. That way you can see at a glance whether the sources agree.

**Change:** “7 days: ▲ 3%”, “30 days: ▼ 8%”.

**Links:**
- **Offers in Spain ↗**: Cardmarket, sellers from Spain only and English cards.
- **Offers in Europe ↗**: Cardmarket, all of Europe and English cards.
- **TCGplayer ↗**: if the card is matched with TCGplayer.

If the card has no product on Cardmarket, the links go to a search by its name.

How each price is chosen, with all its rules, is in [Where each price comes from](precios.md).

### Tournament notices

- **⛔ Banned in official tournaments**: it can't go in the deck or in the side deck.
- **⚠️ Limited: 1 copy max**: 1 copy max between the deck and the side deck.

Both show underneath the date from which they apply.

**🃏 Deck building** sums up the card's special rules:
- Ultimate: 1 copy max.
- [Super Combo]: 4 max in the deck.
- [Dragon Ball]: 7 max in total.

### Official erratum

If Bandai has published an erratum, **✎ Official erratum** appears with its date, two columns **Before** / **After**, and the erratum's notes.

### Your copies

Below the notices is the panel with **your copies** of that card. It says **Not in your collection** or **In your collection: ×3**, plus “1 fake” if you have any fakes.

![Copies panel on the card page, inside My collection](../img/coleccion-ficha.jpg)

**One row per location**, with its type (Deck, Binder, Storage). Fake copies go in their own row, marked **Fake**. In each row:
- **− / +**: removes or adds a copy in that location. Fake rows don't have **+**.
- **Mark as fake** / **It's genuine**: switches **one** copy from genuine to fake or the other way round. If the row has several copies and the note has positions (for example, from the binder), it asks **Which one is the fake?** and offers each position.
- **Move…**: moves copies to another location: “Move [n] to [location] → **Move**”.
- **Note**: free text of up to 500 characters. It's saved when you leave the field. Examples: “page 12” or, for a fake, “AliExpress, June”.

**Add to My collection in [location] → Add** adds a genuine copy in a location where you don't have that card yet. It suggests the last location you used in this browser, or **Unsorted**.

> [!NOTE]
> This panel only appears when the card is on the results page you have open. If you open it from a link or from the printings row and it isn't on that page, it doesn't show. Search for it and open it from the results.

Everything about copies, locations and fakes is in [My collection](mi-coleccion.md).

### Card text

- **Skills:** the words in brackets (**[Barrier]**, **[Activate : Main]**…) are buttons. Hovering shows their official explanation; clicking filters Search by that skill.
- **Attributes:** below the text is the table with everything Bandai provides about the card: Era, Type, Color, Power, Energy, Rarity, Character, Special Trait, Color Cost, Combo Power, Combo Energy…

### Keyword skills and special traits

- **This card's keyword skills:** each skill with its category (Activate Timing, Keyword Skills or Keywords) and its official explanation. Clicking filters by it.
- **Special traits:** the card's Special Traits (Saiyan, Android…). They also filter when you tap them.

### Questions and answers (Q&A)

If the official site has questions about the card, **Q&A N** appears, collapsed. Open it to read them (Q1, Q2…). The ones Bandai marks as recent carry the **new** tag.

### Notes

At the end, Bandai's notes on the card, if there are any.
